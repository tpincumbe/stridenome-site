Stridenome

Stridenome is a background cadence metronome for runners. It plays a steady click under your podcast or audiobook, supports Run/Walk intervals, announces your distance, and lets two friends start a run together.

Support

For help, bug reports or questions, write to stridenome@pincumbe.net. Please tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you did before the problem.

Distance, pace and timing in the app are estimates from your phone's sensors.

Privacy

Stridenome does not collect your data. Read the Privacy Policy.
